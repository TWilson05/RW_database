# Results coverage and handoff

Snapshot: 2026-11-15. The objective is a continually growing Canadian racewalk database, not an assertion that any athlete's career is completely inventoried. Use [context.md](context.md) for project conventions and [the importer guide](importer/README.md) for exact commands and limitations.

## Current state

A live read on this date found 821 athletes, 218 teams, 1,248 races, 3,010 results and 355 splits. These are rows, not counts of Canadian finishers or unique verified performances. Of the races, 545 have a blank meet name, so meet-name searches substantially undercount coverage. An audit flagged six pairs of possibly duplicate athletes and four repeated athlete/race pairs; these are leads, not confirmed duplicates. Re-run the audit on current data and investigate before merging. The latest import read back successfully and the 15 existing importer tests passed.

International results have been researched and imported in successive passes for Olympics (including 1906), World senior track championships, World Race Walking Team Championships, Pan American Race Walking Cup and Pan American Games, NACAC and Pan American track championships across age groups, and selected World U18/U20, Commonwealth, Francophonie and Universiade competitions. These are **researched, partially covered series**, not certified complete. Youth Olympics and FISU were researched in the last pass but no full edition-by-edition coverage ledger was established. Existing results may lack meet names. NAIA and old Athletics Canada historical rankings were previously entered by the user; their completeness has not been independently audited. Athlete-profile follow-up has been useful but is not exhaustive or a prerequisite for closing a meet.

## Coverage assessment and priorities

The user's proposed gaps are sound. Order these by value, access to sources and the ability to reconcile overlapping data; no exact coverage percentage is yet defensible.

1. **Athletics Canada current results, especially 2010 onward:** establish a repeatable source workflow and compare by athlete, date, distance and mark. This will surface international, national and provincial results already entered under incomplete meet names. Do not assume the database contains every race or all DQ/DNF and splits.
2. **Canadian national championships, by edition and category:** senior, U20, U18 and U16, including Legion and other historical names and any changes in championship organization. Confirm which ages and events actually existed in each year. Use official meet results where possible to reconstruct full fields; compare multiple categories sharing a start before creating separate races.
3. **Canada-USA U20 / junior dual meets:** build an edition and event inventory, acknowledging historical naming and age-class changes. Find official meet results and distinguish those competitions from other Canada-USA races.
4. **Provincial high-school results:** inventory the user's existing historical database before choosing import ranges; reconcile names, school years, province, distances and hand/electronic timing. Some records may be incomplete or rankings rather than full races.
5. **Pre-2010 provincial championships and other provincial events:** prioritize provincial federation archives, event reports and historical rankings; link the same meet across sources before importing.
6. **International masters:** inventory World Masters Athletics outdoor/indoor championships and relevant regional championships, plus other masters meets the user wants. Confirm age groups and whether official results list Canada, residence or club; avoid assuming citizenship. Preserve age category and exact surface/distance.
7. **University and other gaps:** audit Canadian university (U SPORTS/CIS and predecessors), NCAA, NAIA, college and conference competitions, and international university games where Canadian athletes appear. NAIA has prior user-entered coverage, so check rather than re-import indiscriminately. Also consider Commonwealth Youth Games, regional/multisport games, and open international road meetings when evidence shows Canadian participation; participation and event existence vary by edition.
8. **Finish the international ledger:** revisit each series already researched, including Youth Olympics, FISU and Francophonie, with an edition-by-edition list and source links. Mark each edition as checked with Canadian entries imported, checked with none, unavailable, or unresolved. Include the source scope and date checked; never infer completeness from a few searched records.

For a practical next session, start with one bounded set, such as the latest three years of Athletics Canada rankings and their national championship results. Build the ledger alongside importing, then report checked editions, omissions, additions, corrections and unresolved claims.

## How to continue safely

1. Read [context.md](context.md), [importer/README.md](importer/README.md) and [examples/import.json](examples/import.json). Retrieve a fresh sheet snapshot; do not rely on this handoff's counts. Credentials and spreadsheet ID are in ignored `secrets/`; never print or commit them.
2. Agent researches sources and prepares grouped structured race data. The code does not search the web, infer identity or determine authoritative sources by itself. Record source links, raw values, assumptions and decisions in the input/plan or a durable research ledger. Plans and automatic backups are ignored private files, not a permanent shared provenance database.
3. Search existing athletes, aliases, races and results; use the duplicate checker and review near-date and equal-mark candidates. Blank or inconsistent meet fields and alternate locations defeat simple searches. Treat all automatic warnings as leads, not proof. Keep international fields to Canadian representatives unless full results are requested; exclude DNS and relays.
4. Plan; review operations, errors and audit warnings; resolve factual conflicts per [context.md](context.md). Apply only a fresh plan, avoid concurrent sheet edits, inspect the backup after any uncertain failure, and confirm read-back. Data changes are authorized by the user's continuing import requests, subject to clarification when identities or separate events cannot be established.
5. Keep source-specific coverage and open verification notes current. Minor mark or spelling disputes should normally have a provisional choice plus a note, not block adding a result. An unverified alternate spelling is not a confirmed alias for automatic merging.

## Current data caveats

- The 1986 Pan American junior 3,000 m result for Édith Montpetit is provisionally 15:34.45 (another compilation says 15:34.35); the 1982 Pan American junior athlete is provisionally Luc Cauvet (another source says Gauvet); François Lapointe's 1980 46:55.88 remains in place (another compilation says 46:55.00). Race notes record these for later checking against original or Canadian/Quebec sources. The NACAC-hosted tables used in two cases are compilations, not confirmed original results.
- Guillaume Leblanc's 1985 Kobe Universiade 20 km and a same-date 10 km result both exist. The 10 km could be a split, but that relationship is unverified; investigate before moving or deleting it.
- The 1966 and 1970 Commonwealth walks are stored as 32.18688 km (20 miles). The current dashboard has a fixed distance selector, so an arbitrary distance might not be visible in its usual rankings until the display is expanded.
- The dashboard currently drops DQ/DNF from profiles and splits of those results, identifies some views by names instead of stable IDs, and has limited distance options. The desired display treatment of splits from DQ/DNF remains open. Ingestion success does not mean these display issues are fixed.
- The importer uses heuristics and has no transactional lock or general structured provenance/alias registry. Its backups, exact snapshot checks, read-back and tests reduce risk but cannot establish source accuracy or completeness. Preserve unknown data and partial dates; use assumed last meet day only when appropriate and clearly annotated.
